About APSHULE

APSHULE is a Ugandan digital learning platform built to make quality education accessible to every student across Uganda. Whether you're a primary pupil, secondary student, or school administrator, APSHULE has the tools you need — live classes, study materials, and powerful school management features, all on your phone.
