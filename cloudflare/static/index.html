Made for real teams

Practical workflows, with clear access boundaries.

People see the records and actions their role is authorized to use.

🎓 Student and teacher access

Students see linked academic records. Teachers work with classes and subjects assigned to their account.

🏫 School operations

Authorized school staff manage admissions, learners, attendance, marks, and bursar records.

🏥 Care and pharmacy

Clinic teams coordinate appointments, visits, prescriptions, stock, checkout, billing, and claims.

🌱 Farm records

Farm teams record animals, movements, attendance, eggs, inventory, and online feed use.

🏦 Lending workflows

MFI teams handle borrower, collateral, loan application, and approval records within their scope.

🛡️ Super Admin controls

Authorized administrators manage user roles and scopes and review platform audit activity.
