Task 15 — Bug Fixes (Two Issues):

BUG 1 — SUBSCRIPTION PLAN NOT WORKING
The subscription plans on the Home screen are not functioning:
- Tapping a plan does not open the payment flow
- Nothing happens when a user taps Subscribe
- Some users see "Online payment is coming soon" or nothing at all

Fix:
- Make each subscription plan tappable
- On tap, open the payment modal
- Payment modal should show:
  * Plan name and price
  * Payment method selector (MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money)
  * Phone number input
  * Continue button
- If Yo! Payments is not configured, show a clear message:
  "Please contact your school admin to subscribe"
- Ensure the plan price shown matches the plan selected
- Test: tap Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Term, Half Year, Full Year 
  and confirm each opens the payment flow correctly

BUG 2 — NOTIFICATIONS STILL APPEAR IN TOP RIGHT CORNER
Contextual messages are still showing in the top right corner as 
floating toasts when a user taps on a specific item. They should 
appear ON or NEAR the tapped item instead.

Fix for ALL contextual messages:
- Move the message to appear directly below or on top of the 
  tapped element
- Use a small tooltip or inline banner at the tap location
- Auto-hide after about 3 seconds with a smooth fade
- Do NOT use the top-right toast for messages tied to a 
  specific element

Apply this to ALL contextual cases:
- Tapping a class with no subjects
- Tapping a subject with no video
- Tapping a subscription plan
- Tapping a resource with no PDF
- Tapping a live lesson with no enrollment
- Tapping any feature that is empty or unavailable

Keep the top-right toast ONLY for global system messages like:
- Sync complete
- Payment confirmed
- Login successful
- Network status changes

TESTING:
1. Tap any subscription plan — payment modal or clear message opens
2. Tap a class with 0 subjects — message appears under the class card
3. Tap a subject with no video — message appears next to the subject
4. Confirm no contextual messages show in the top right corner
5. Confirm global messages still work in the top right

Do NOT rebuild the app. Do NOT break Tasks 1 through 15.

contextual messages appear at tap location"
