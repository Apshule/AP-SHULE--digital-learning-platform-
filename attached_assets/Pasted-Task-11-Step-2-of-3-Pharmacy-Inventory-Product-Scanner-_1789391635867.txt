COLOR HISTOGRAM STORAGE:
When a product image is uploaded:
- Compute 16-bin RGB histogram (48 numbers total)
- Store in productEmbedding field
- Used for future matching

This is a lightweight, offline-friendly approach that doesn't 
require Gemini Vision or any paid API.

FALLBACK:
If matching confidence < 40%:
- Show "No good match found"
- Show [⌨️ Enter Manually] option
- Log scan to clinic_product_scans with scanMethod="Manual"

PART J: INSURANCE CLAIM WORKFLOW
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

When a patient has insurance:

1. During billing, mark bill as insurance-covered:
   - Insurance Provider
   - Policy Number
   - Coverage % (e.g. 80%)
   - Patient Copay % (e.g. 20%)

2. Auto-calculate:
   - Insurance Coverage = Total × 80%
   - Patient Copay = Total × 20%

3. Create insurance claim:
   - Status: Draft
   - Claim Amount = Insurance Coverage
   - Patient Copay = Patient portion

4. Workflow:
   Draft → Submitted → Under Review → Approved / Rejected → Paid

5. When [📤 Submit to Insurer]:
   - Update status to Submitted
   - Notify clinic admin
   - Generate claim PDF

6. When [✅ Record Approval]:
   - Enter approved amount (may be less than claimed)
   - Enter approval date
   - Update bill:
     * If approved fully: bill.paymentStatus = "Paid"
     * If approved partially: remaining becomes patient's responsibility
   - Notify patient of approval

7. When [💰 Mark as Paid]:
   - Insurance payment arrives
   - Create clinic_payments with method = "Insurance"
   - Update bill
