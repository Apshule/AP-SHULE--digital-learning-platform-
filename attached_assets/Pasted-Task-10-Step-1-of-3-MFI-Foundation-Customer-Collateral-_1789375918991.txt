PART H: AUTO-SCORING ENGINE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The heart of Task 10 — automatic collateral scoring.

Add function computeCollateralScore(collateralData, customerData):
Returns { assetTypeScore, ownershipProofScore, marketValueScore, 
          locationScore, liquidityScore, totalScore, grade, recommendation }

SCORING RUBRIC:

1. Asset Type (0-25 points):
   Based on collateral.typeId (from mfi_collateral_types.baseScore):
   - Land/Property: 25
   - Vehicle: 20
   - Guarantor: 15
   - Business Assets: 12
   - Bank Guarantee: 20
   - Household Assets: 5
   
   Take the baseScore directly from the type record.

2. Ownership Proof (0-25 points):
   - Official Title/Logbook: 25
   - Signed Agreement: 15
   - Bank Statement: 20
   - No proof / None: 0

3. Market Value (0-20 points):
   Compare estimatedValueUgx to customer's total requested loan 
   amount (if available; otherwise use customer.monthlyIncome * 12):
   - Value >= 150% of reference: 20
   - 100-149%: 15
   - 50-99%: 10
   - Below 50%: 0

4. Location/Accessibility (0-15 points):
   Based on location text. Check for keywords:
   - Kampala/Wakiso/Entebbe: 15
   - Regional Town (Mbarara, Gulu, Mbale, Jinja, etc.): 10
   - Other rural: 5
   - Empty/Unknown: 0

5. Liquidity (0-15 points):
   Based on asset type:
   - Land/Property: 15 (easy to sell)
   - Vehicle: 12
   - Bank Guarantee: 15
   - Business Assets: 8
   - Guarantor: 5
   - Household Assets: 3

TOTAL SCORE = sum of all 5 categories (max 100)

INTERPRETATION:
- 80-100: Grade "Excellent" → Recommendation "Approve"
- 60-79: Grade "Good" → Recommendation "Approve"
- 40-59: Grade "Fair" → Recommendation "Request Top-up"
- Below 40: Grade "Weak" → Recommendation "Reject"

Store all in mfi_collateral_scoring.
