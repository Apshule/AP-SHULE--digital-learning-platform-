PART C: OVERDUE MONITORING + ESCALATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Daily process (runs every night at 11 PM):

STEP 1 — Find overdue installments:
- Query mfi_loan_repayment_schedule WHERE dueDate < today AND status != 'paid'
- For each: compute daysOverdue

STEP 2 — Compute late fees:
- Check mfi_late_fee_config for the institution
- If daysOverdue > gracePeriodDays:
  * If Fixed: lateFee = config.lateFeeAmount
  * If Percentage: lateFee = overdueAmount × (percentage/100)
  * Cap at config.maxLateFeeCap
  * Update installment.lateFeeApplied
  * Update installment.status = 'overdue'

STEP 3 — Escalation based on days:
- Day 1-3: Send SMS reminder (gentle)
- Day 4-7: Send SMS + in-app (firm)
- Day 8-14: Send SMS + call + email (warning)
- Day 15-30: SMS + call + field visit scheduled
- Day 31-60: Final demand letter
- Day 61+: Legal action referral

STEP 4 — Log to mfi_loan_overdue_log:
- Record each action taken
- Update customer's loan status = 'overdue'

STEP 5 — Notify parties:
- Customer (SMS + in-app)
- Loan Officer (in-app)
- Manager (in-app if >30 days)

PART F: LOAN RESTRUCTURING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

When customer can't pay, MFI can restructure:

TRIGGERS:
- 3+ missed installments
- Customer requests (hardship)
- Business downturn
- Natural disaster

WORKFLOW:
1. Loan Officer creates restructure request with:
   - Reason (financial_hardship / business_downturn / 
     natural_disaster / other)
   - Requested new terms (extended term, reduced interest, 
     reduced monthly payment)
   - Justification (textarea)
2. Manager reviews
3. Director approves

IMPACT:
- Remaining balance distributed over NEW term
- New interest rate applies
- New repayment schedule generated
- Old schedule marked as 'restructured' (kept for history)
- New expected payback date
