PART H: ERROR HANDLING & EDGE CASES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Handle these gracefully:

1. Video URL is broken:
   - Show "Video unavailable. Contact admin." 
   - Allow marking lesson complete with admin override

2. PDF URL is broken:
   - Show "PDF not available. Read the summary instead."
   - Allow marking complete after 10-second delay

3. Quiz has missing questions:
   - Show "Quiz being updated. Try again later."

4. Practical upload fails:
   - Retry 2 times
   - If still fails: "Save photo offline and retry"

5. Certificate generation fails:
   - Retry 3 times
   - Log error to Firestore error_logs collection
   - Show teacher: "Certificate being prepared. Check back in 5 minutes."

6. Teacher loses internet mid-module:
   - Auto-save progress every 10 seconds
   - Resume where left off when reconnected
