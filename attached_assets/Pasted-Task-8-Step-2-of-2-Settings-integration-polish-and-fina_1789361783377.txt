PART H: PERSIST SETTINGS ACROSS DEVICES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

When user logs in on a new device:

1. Fetch users/{uid}/preferences from Firestore
2. If preferences exist:
   - Merge with local offline_settings
   - Firestore wins for account-level prefs (language, theme)
   - IndexedDB wins for device-level prefs (data saver, download quality)
3. Apply all preferences immediately
4. Show toast if settings were synced: "Settings synced from your account"

When user changes a setting:
1. Save to IndexedDB immediately
2. Save to Firestore users/{uid}/preferences
3. If offline, queue for sync
